Research

My research is in applied cryptography, centered on lattices. I design post-quantum protocols that are both provably secure and practical, and so far have worked on fully homomorphic encryption, metadata-private messaging, leader election for blockchains, and verifiable computation over encrypted data. Going forward, I am excited about advanced public-key encryption, new lattice-based primitives, and post-quantum cryptography and SNARKs more broadly, toward a future where strong cryptography is quantum-safe, expressive, and verifiable by default.
